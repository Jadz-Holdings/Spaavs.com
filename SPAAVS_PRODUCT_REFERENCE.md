# SPAAVS persistent product terminology and operating principles

Read this reference before any SPAAVS modification. Preserve these distinctions unless the user explicitly approves a change.

The AI-integrated master business and investor package, `SPAAVS_Master_Business_Investor_AI.zip`, is confidential persistent memory. Only a master admin may retrieve or distribute it. Do not commit, deploy, or publish the archive or its financial figures.

- **SPAAVS Mobile App:** Customer-facing application for human drivers.
- **SPAAVS Fleet Hub:** The website commercial fleet operators use to subscribe to SPAAVS services, pay for them, and manage their autonomous vehicles. It is the fleet operator interface and the authorized system-to-system integration solution for parking, charging, tracking, appointments, and readiness. It replaces both SPAAVS Mobile Hub and SPAAVS Fleet API as branded names. Do not reintroduce either name, and do not list both as separate products.
- **SPAAVS Fleet Center:** A center at every facility, staffed by people on site. It is not the Fleet Hub website and not the SPAAVS Mobility Hub. Do not rename it Service Center. The center can check tire pressure and add air, complete a 360-degree visual inspection, provide basic lubrication when required, top off windshield-washer fluid, provide a car wash, and detail the interior. A vehicle from a fleet that subscribes to SPAAVS services may use another facility on the network when circumstances require it, typically in an emergency, such as when a reserved space is unavailable. Do not brand that access as a separate Guest Hub.
- **SPAAVS Mobility Hub:** Official name of the Azure-hosted SaaS technology stack. It is not a physical facility and not a replacement label for the Fleet Hub. The Four-Tier Facility Development Model still uses this name for Tier 4. Do not rename that tier unless the user approves a change.
- **SPAAVS Core Platform Services:** Architectural component of the SPAAVS Mobility Hub, paired with SPAAVS Facility and Fleet Connectivity. It is not a separately branded commercial product. Core Platform Services remain authoritative for reservations, access, assignments, payments, and service coordination.
- **SPAAVS Facility and Fleet Connectivity:** Architectural component of the SPAAVS Mobility Hub, paired with SPAAVS Core Platform Services. It is not a separately branded commercial product.
- **SPAAVS AI Intelligence Layer:** A shared layer inside the Azure SPAAVS Mobility Hub. It supports the SPAAVS Mobile App now and can support the SPAAVS Fleet Hub later. It provides conversational voice and text, predictive occupancy, consent-based personalization, proactive assistance, and headless workflow orchestration. It is not a separate product and it does not replace Core Platform Services or Facility and Fleet Connectivity.
- **Integrated LiDAR and Camera Infrastructure:** Facility-based technology supporting vehicle identification, tracking, parking availability and indoor navigation.
- **Indoor Mapping Technology:** Spatial representation of participating facilities supporting navigation and route guidance.

APIs are underlying technical interfaces, not branded commercial products. Do not rename API endpoints, database identifiers, or internal software interfaces because of this branding change. Do not imply that customer phones require LiDAR. BLE/UWB are contingency options, not baseline infrastructure. Vehicle identification matches an arriving vehicle to its visit. Entry is confirmed with the reservation and authorized credentials. On public pages, explain it that way. Do not say that a license plate alone is not authorization. Autonomous driving remains under the vehicle manufacturer's control; SPAAVS coordinates facility services through authorized integrations.

Parking reservations and vehicle service appointments are optional for human drivers. Walk-ins are subject to availability and facility rules. Services can be requested at participating facilities or through the Mobile App, independently or together. No mandatory bundles or app-only service requirements. Preserve hands-free driver guidance. The SPAAVS Mobile App is designed to accept a spoken, hands-free service request so the driver does not look at or hold the phone while driving. Spoken indoor guidance uses facility cameras, LiDAR, and indoor mapping. Do not imply that the phone requires LiDAR.

Public AI copy describes intended capabilities. Do not present the assistant, predictions, or headless workflows as live deployed functionality. Hands-free use is designed to reduce manual interaction. It does not eliminate distraction, and drivers remain responsible for safe driving. Do not claim quantified safety results. Predictions are estimates. Do not publish prices or financial estimates for AI examples.

Headless execution is speech recognition, then intent extraction, then an authenticated session, then a read-only availability or status lookup, then a validated quote, then explicit authorization for a paid or consequential request, then platform execution, then a spoken confirmation only after the platform confirms the result. Never announce success before confirmation. Defer complex or visual tasks until the vehicle is parked. No direct model access to payment credentials. No autonomous vehicle control. Use validated routing and live sensor data, not model-generated driving directions. Say when facility data is missing or stale. Do not infer consent. Preferences, calendar, location, and vehicle information require permission, with review, deletion, and opt-out. Describe intended capabilities without claiming verified deployments or partnerships.
